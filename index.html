how it works

Three steps. Your secrets never go in.

The AI still understands your question, because it sees a clear label in place of each secret.

  1. TICKET1

    Hand them in

    Paste your text. Every secret becomes a ticket like {{EMAIL_1}}. Copy the safe version.

  2. TICKET2

    Ask your AI

    Paste the safe version into any AI chat. It answers using the tickets and never sees the real values.

  3. TICKET3

    Collect them after

    Paste the reply into Restore. Every ticket turns back into your real value, ready to use.

good questions

Before you start

Is it really free?

Yes. Every tool on this site is free to use, with no account.

Does it work with ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini?

Yes. You copy the safe text and paste it into any AI chat, so it works with all of them.

Will it catch every secret?

No tool can promise that. CoatCheck AI highlights everything it replaces, so take a quick look before you send. You can also add your own private words.

What if I close the tab before restoring?

The list that links tickets to real values is kept only until you close the tab. Save it as a file from the Key map if you want to restore a reply later.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit it keeps working without internet, and you can install it like an app from your browser.

Next time, check your secrets in first.

It takes a few seconds, and your real data stays with you.
