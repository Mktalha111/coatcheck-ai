        How CoatCheck AI works

        Like a coat check: hand in your secrets, get a ticket, collect them after.

        The round trip

        1. Sanitize: paste your text, copy the safe version.
        2. Ask ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini with it.
        3. Restore: paste the reply, copy it back with your real values.

        The key map that links placeholders to values stays in this tab. Save it as a file if you want to restore a reply later.

        Privacy

        • Everything runs in this page. Nothing is sent anywhere.
        • A Content-Security-Policy blocks the app from making network requests.
        • The only things saved between visits are your settings. Your text and key map are not, unless you turn on "remember until I close this tab".

        • Token counts are estimates (about ±15%). Real tokenizers differ by model.
