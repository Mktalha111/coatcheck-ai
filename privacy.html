Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

The short version We collect nothing. Everything you paste into CoatCheck AI is processed on your own device and never sent to us or anyone else. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no ads.

What happens to your text

CoatCheck AI runs entirely in your web browser. When you paste text, files or an AI reply, the scanning, replacing and restoring all happen on your device. The page has a security policy (a "Content-Security-Policy") that blocks it from sending data to any server, so even a bug could not upload your text.

What is stored on your device

  • Your settings (theme, which detectors are on, placeholder style, compress and bundle options, the price model you picked) are kept in your browser's local storage so they are there next time. They contain no text you pasted.
  • The key map (the list that links placeholders like {{EMAIL_1}} to your real values) and your private words are kept in this tab's session storage only if "Remember it until I close this tab" is on. Your browser deletes session storage when you close the tab. Turn the option off to keep them in memory only.
  • App files are cached by your browser so CoatCheck AI works offline. They contain no personal data.
  • Key map files you choose to save are stored wherever you put them. They contain your real secrets, so keep them private.

You can delete everything at any time in your browser's settings (site data) or with "Forget all" in the key map.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up, no accounts, no email collection.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising.
  • No third-party scripts or fonts. Even the fonts are served from our own site, so nothing is loaded from Google or anyone else.
  • We never see, sell or share what you paste, because it never reaches us.

Hosting

The website is hosted by Cloudflare. Like every web host, Cloudflare may automatically record basic technical information when your browser downloads the site (such as IP address, browser type, time and the page requested) to keep the service secure and running. We don't use this information to identify you. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Children

CoatCheck AI is a general tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If CoatCheck AI ever adds an optional paid account, its terms will be explained here before anything is collected.
